## Worst shapes

Most severe for kuib first. Severity: HIGH when the plan has the shape or leaves it open,
MEDIUM when a ruling avoids it but an item as written could bring it back, LOW when it does
not apply and stands as a warning. References use the short names defined under
[Per subject](#per-subject). Where a finding touches a decision that carries a ruling, it is
stated as a risk that ruling carries and what can be added beside it, never as a reversal.

### W01 · HIGH · Opaque provider data that does not record who issued it

- **Shape.** A signature or an encrypted blob is stored in the conversation without the
  identity of the endpoint that issued it, so it can be sent to an endpoint that cannot
  validate it.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter D; Portkey D; LiteLLM proxy (secondary).
- **Evidence.** OpenRouter tags each reasoning detail with a `format` that names a wire family
  (`google-gemini-v1`), not the issuer (`tssdk:src/models/reasoningformat.ts:11-19`). A tool-call
  continuation routed from Vertex to AI Studio, or back, fails; the maintainer: "Gemini thought
  signatures are provider-scoped, so they can't be validated by a different upstream provider"
  and "The SDK can't reliably prevent this, since OpenRouter selects the upstream Google
  provider on its backend" (#491, 2026-06-17). The client can only filter by `format`
  (`aisdk:src/chat/convert-to-openrouter-chat-messages.ts:308-320`) and names "switching between
  models mid-conversation" as a cause of signature loss (`:353-361`). Portkey has no code that
  checks which provider produced a signature (not found) and on a fallback re-runs the next
  provider's table over the same body (`gw:src/handlers/handlerUtils.ts:664-670`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** 400 on the continuation, "Unable to submit request because Thought signature is not
  valid" / "Corrupted thought signature", shown to the user as "Provider returned error" (#491).
  The Anthropic variant took four pull requests and a reopened issue between 2026-02-19 and
  2026-04-08 (#423, #453; `aisdk:e2e/issues/issue-453-signature-reopen.test.ts:5-13`). A
  LiteLLM router that mixes Anthropic with another provider fails on multi-turn thinking and
  "adding the drop_params or modify_params doesn't fix" it
  (`github.com/BerriAI/litellm/discussions/31973`, 2026-07-02, unanswered by maintainers).
- **Does the plan share it?** Undecided. G006 settles only where the payload may sit. P02-I02
  names "the opaque provider payload" with no issuer. The offering switch "how reasoning can be
  replayed" (P02-I01) describes the offering that receives, not the one that issued. D004 gives a
  session a provider and a model, and nothing says they cannot change between two steps.
- **What kuib should do.** Stamp every payload with the offering that produced it, as part of
  the payload's shape in P02-I02. The "send reasoning back" block compares the stamp with the
  offering being asked, drops the payload on a mismatch and reports it (W02). What scopes
  validity is to be measured, not assumed: the Gemini case shows the provider is part of it;
  whether a Claude signature issued by Anthropic is accepted by the same model at another
  provider was not found. Add to P05-I01 a fixture in which a closed step from one offering is
  placed in a step asked of another (Claude to MiMo and back).

### W02 · HIGH · An unsupported intent is dropped and nobody is told

- **Shape.** The layer builds the provider's request from what the provider's table knows; what
  the caller asked beyond that disappears, and the answer is a 200.
- **Subject and axis.** Portkey G; OpenRouter G; the providers themselves.
- **Evidence.** Portkey walks the provider's table, not the request (`for (const configParam in
  providerConfig) { … if (configParam in params)`,
  `gw:src/services/transformToProviderRequest.ts:83-92`); nothing reports a dropped parameter (not
  found). OpenRouter: "If the chosen model doesn't support a request parameter … then the
  parameter is ignored" (`ordocs:api_reference/overview`), `require_parameters` defaults to
  `false` (`ordocs:guides/routing/provider-selection`). Providers do it too: Bedrock "accepts the
  field but silently ignores it" (`ordocs:guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens`), and MiMo
  removes a `tool_choice` other than `auto`
  ([[features/engine-foundation/research/engine-design-2026-10-07]], from MiMo's documentation).
  CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** Portkey #1579, open since 2026-03-25: cache marks never reach Claude on Vertex, so
  users "cannot use prompt caching, which significantly increases costs and latency". #1639:
  zero reasoning tokens, "HTTP 200 with no error or warning". #1672: "The caller receives a 200
  with a plausible-looking response". OpenRouter #508: a Gemini cache mark was dropped whenever
  `tools` were present, found only by asking for the upstream body.
- **Their own correction.** OpenRouter added a caller-set strictness (`require_parameters`) and,
  for a change of effort in the middle of a conversation, chose to fail: "rejected with a 400
  instead of being sent with the update silently dropped". They ended up needing both
  behaviours.
- **Does the plan share it?** Partly. D003 rules that a request does not fail for a missing
  feature and that the translation "reports what it did". The first half is this shape; the
  second half is the cure, and it has no home in the plan: P02-I02's closed step holds parts, an
  ending, usage and the payload, and P02-I03's port yields deltas and a closed step. What the
  provider itself ignored is outside what a translation can report from the request alone.
- **What kuib should do.** Inside D003: add to P02-I02 a typed record on the closed step, one
  entry per intent, saying how it was rendered (as asked, degraded to what, dropped), each an
  enum. Because the offering's switches are data, the same record can be returned by P03-I03's
  "builds the exact request without sending it", before any call. Where the response can show
  that the provider ignored something (a cache hint with no cache read or write in usage,
  reasoning asked and none returned), record it as not observed. Risk the ruling carries: a
  caller that depends on an intent (a forced tool choice) cannot ask for a failure instead;
  whether a step may mark an intent as required is a question for the owner.

### W03 · HIGH · Identity has two fields and the thing served varies on more

- **Shape.** A model is named by a provider and a model while what is actually served also
  differs by region, tier, snapshot date and host; the extra facts go into the string, or have
  nowhere to go.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter A; Portkey A; models.dev A and its consumer; Helicone and
  Cloudflare AI Gateway (secondary).
- **Evidence.** One OpenRouter string encodes author, family, tier (`:free`, `:batch`), routing
  policy (`:nitro`, `:floor`, `:exacto`), a moving alias (`~…-latest`) and, in a second field, a
  date; one model has eight endpoints over five providers with the region in the endpoint tag
  (`orapi`). Portkey strips seven region prefixes by regex (`gw:src/providers/bedrock/utils.ts:544-546`)
  and reads the vendor from the first dot segment. opencode, reading models.dev, skips six
  region prefixes and `arn:` ids (`oc:packages/opencode/src/provider/provider.ts:412-420`) and
  turns a catalogue id into a native slug by `replaceAll(".", "-")`, justified by "No native
  Anthropic slug contains a dot, so the blanket replacement is lossless here" (`:903-908`).
  Helicone's `model` string is a routing program: `"gpt-4o-mini/azure/clm1a2b3c"`,
  `"gpt-4o-mini/azure,gpt-4o-mini/openai,gpt-4o-mini"`, `"!openai,gpt-4o-mini"`
  (`docs.helicone.ai/gateway/provider-routing`). models.dev, with no field for region or plan,
  multiplies providers instead: Xiaomi is four of them, `xiaomi` and
  `xiaomi-token-plan-{ams,cn,sgp}`, each a different host, and Claude Sonnet 4.5 has 24
  distinct ids across its providers (`md:providers/`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** Features encoded in a slug die with it: `:extended` is deprecated and "Requests to a
  `model:extended` slug have no endpoints to route to and will fail". Clients that parse the
  string get it wrong: "Do not strip every suffix. Stripping `:free` resolves to the paid
  entry, which can report a larger context window"
  (`ordocs:guides/routing/model-variants/overview`). A catalogue keyed by file name registered
  `qwen/qwen-3.6-27b` for `qwen/qwen3.6-27b`: "Calls using the registry name fail with `is not a
  valid model ID`" (models.dev #1753, open). An alias changes the model under a session and
  remaps its parameters: "`~latest` slugs trade exactness for continuity"
  (`ordocs:guides/routing/routers/latest-resolution`).
- **Does the plan share it?** Partly. D004 rules the free string out, and the failures above are
  the case for that ruling. What remains: the offering is keyed by provider and model and holds
  one native slug (D005, P02-I01), so a second way of serving the same pair has no row. kuib
  already has such a pair: MiMo at Xiaomi is served from a token-plan host and from
  `api.xiaomimimo.com` (G008; D017 has "the MiMo key tried on both Xiaomi hosts"). A dated
  snapshot, a region and a batch tier are the next ones. G004 is open on the neighbouring
  question.
- **What kuib should do.** Decide in P02-I01, before the enums are written, which of snapshot
  date, region, tier and host are identity and which are data on a row, and whether one
  provider and model may have more than one offering. Keep the native slug a literal on the
  row; never compute it by editing a string. This is a gap to put to the owner beside G004, not
  a change to D004 or D005.

### W04 · HIGH · The ending is a short closed list and the provider's own reason is lost

- **Shape.** Every provider's stop reason is folded into a few values, an unknown one becomes the
  normal one, and the original is discarded or overwritten.
- **Subject and axis.** Portkey F and E; OpenRouter F.
- **Evidence.** Portkey: one flat map to four values; "if (!transformedFinishReason) return
  FINISH_REASON.stop" (`gw:src/providers/utils.ts:77-82`); Anthropic `pause_turn`, Google
  `MALFORMED_FUNCTION_CALL`, DeepSeek `insufficient_system_resource` and Mistral `ERROR` all
  become `stop` (`gw:src/providers/utils/finishReasonMap.ts:17-121`); a provider `error` event is
  written into `finish_reason` with its message discarded
  (`gw:src/providers/anthropic/chatComplete.ts:673-691`); a Bedrock exception becomes `stop`
  (`gw:src/providers/bedrock/utils.ts:530-537`). OpenRouter keeps the original beside the
  normalised value (`native_finish_reason`), and its client still has to rewrite the reason
  from the content (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:455-475`, `:1141-1159`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** "Some models return `finish_reason: null` in streaming responses even when tool
  calls are present … breaking agentic loops" (#166, `aisdk:e2e/issues/issue-166-finish-reason-null.test.ts:5-8`;
  again #420). Portkey had to "append fake finish_reason to last response stream chunk"
  (`gw@b52ee0e`). A failed generation presented as a normal stop is indistinguishable from a
  finished one (INFERRED from the two Portkey transforms).
- **Does the plan share it?** Partly. D013 keeps tool calls as parts and the provider's id as
  data, so whether tools must run is read from the parts and not from a reason, which avoids the
  loop failure. But P02-I02 says the closed step has "one of five endings" (completed,
  interrupted, failed, cut off by a limit, abandoned) and names no place for the provider's
  reason. A refusal, a content filter, `pause_turn`, and MiMo's documented `content_filter` and
  `repetition_truncation` must each become one of the five, and the original is gone.
- **What kuib should do.** Keep the provider's reason verbatim on the closed step, as data
  beside the ending. Make the mapping from reason to ending a table in the translation with an
  explicit entry for "not recognised" that is not "completed". Put to the owner where a refusal
  and a content filter belong among the five. P01 already records a rejected request; add a
  capture that ends in a reason other than end of turn, tool use or the token limit.

### W05 · HIGH · One provider's vocabulary serves as the neutral intent

- **Shape.** The neutral request names cache and reasoning in the words of the first provider
  that had the feature, and every other provider gets a lossy conversion.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter C; Portkey C.
- **Evidence.** OpenRouter's cache intent is `cache_control: { type: 'ephemeral' }` in three
  placements, and "TTLs are not translated — a `cache_control` `ttl` is dropped toward OpenAI";
  reasoning is either `effort` or `max_tokens`, converted by fixed shares
  (`ordocs:guides/best-practices/{prompt-caching,reasoning-tokens}`). Portkey rewrites any truthy
  mark to `{ type: 'ephemeral' }`, so a lifetime is lost
  (`gw:src/providers/anthropic/chatComplete.ts:341-357`), drives Gemini with Anthropic's
  `thinking` shape (`gw:src/providers/google/chatComplete.ts:82-88`) and forwards OpenAI's raw
  `reasoning_effort` value as Gemini's `thinkingLevel` (`:95-98`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** A message-level mark was applied to every part, "exceeding Anthropic's 4-segment
  limit" (#341); a mark on a system string was not applied at all (#389); automatic caching
  could not be asked for (#424) (`aisdk:e2e/issues/issue-{341,389,424}-*.test.ts`, header
  comments). Through Portkey a one-hour mark becomes a five-minute one with no notice (INFERRED
  from the rewrite).
- **Does the plan share it?** Undecided. P02-I02 asks for "hints for caching and instruction
  lifetime" and "the reasoning level for this step" without saying what a hint or a level is.
  The variants drawn so far (MARKERS · IMPLICIT · NONE; EFFORT · BUDGET · TOGGLE · NONE) are
  named after what providers have, which is right for blocks and says nothing yet about the
  neutral side. Open: is a cache hint a position, a lifetime, or both; who holds Anthropic's
  limit of four marks; and, if the level is a neutral enum, where the number for a BUDGET model
  lives.
- **What kuib should do.** In the paper check of P02-I02, write for each of the two intents the
  neutral value set and, per variant, the table it maps through. Lifetime as an enum that a
  provider's durations map onto, the unmapped case reported (W02). The budget for a BUDGET model
  as data on the offering row, not a share fixed in code. The mark limit enforced in the
  "place cache marks" block, with the dropped marks reported.

### W06 · HIGH · The same opaque data is reachable from several places

- **Shape.** One provider payload is attached to the message, to a tool call and to a reasoning
  part, with a priority order to pick one when sending back.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter D and E; Portkey D.
- **Evidence.** "Priority: message-level > first tool call > first reasoning part"
  (`aisdk:src/chat/convert-to-openrouter-chat-messages.ts:268-281`); the same details also travel
  on the finish event; the wire has `reasoning`, `reasoning_details` and the alias
  `reasoning_content`. Portkey has three placements for three providers (see Per subject, D).
  CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** The same reasoning id sent twice is rejected, "Duplicate item found with id" (#254,
  `aisdk:src/chat/convert-to-openrouter-chat-messages.ts:50-54`). Each delta carried a full copy
  of everything so far, O(N²) (#413, `aisdk:e2e/issues/issue-413-reasoning-metadata-bloat.test.ts:5-7`).
  One shared array mutated in place put `signature: undefined` on every copy and broke the next
  request (#540, open).
- **Does the plan share it?** Undecided. G006's recommendation is "Allow the payload on any
  model-authored part", which permits this unless it also says once. D010 asks for a shape that
  is "non-duplicated".
- **What kuib should do.** Answer G006 with: a payload belongs to exactly one part, the one the
  provider attached it to (the thinking block for Claude; the tool-call part for Gemini), and is
  never copied to the step or to a sibling. The delta that carries a payload names its part.

### W07 · MEDIUM · Closed enums and closed schemas on the path that reads the provider

- **Shape.** The type that receives a provider's answer admits only the values known when it was
  written.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter J and E.
- **Evidence.** The hand-written client validates the reasoning `format` with a closed enum of
  six values (`aisdk:src/schemas/format.ts:1-8`, `aisdk:src/schemas/reasoning-details.ts:14`) while
  the API has nine (`tssdk:src/models/reasoningformat.ts:11-19`); a detail that fails is turned
  into `null` and filtered out (`aisdk:src/schemas/reasoning-details.ts:58-65`). OpenRouter's
  generated SDK went the other way: every inbound enum is an `OpenEnum` whose unknown values
  are kept as `Unrecognized`, and every outbound enum is `z.string()`
  (`tssdk:src/types/enums.ts:11-40`); its provider enum has 114 members. models.dev made the same
  move between its two schemas: v1 is strict, and in v2 an "enum" is `z.union([z.enum(values),
  z.string()])` (`md:packages/core/src/schema-v2.ts:19-21`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** Responses failed validation whenever the API added a field, until "Add
  `.passthrough()` to all zod object schemas to prevent validation failures"
  (`aisdk:CHANGELOG.md:657-659`). `AI_TypeValidationError` when a provider omitted `arguments`
  (#287). Reasoning details in the three newer formats are dropped without a word (INFERRED from
  the two enums).
- **Does the plan share it?** Partly. The principle "nothing is validated between layers inside
  the process" and P04-I02's "ignores frame kinds it does not know" avoid it for frames. D015
  ("enums everywhere") and D011 ("no open records") do not say what happens to an unknown value
  inside a known frame (a new stop reason, a new block type), or to a stored session that
  carries an enum value the reading build lacks; G007 covers only values authored by plugins.
- **What kuib should do.** State in P02 that an enum kuib authors (requests, switches, endings)
  is closed, and that every enum read from a provider or from storage has an explicit
  "not recognised" arm that keeps the raw string. Extend G007 to core values newer than the
  reader. Risk D004 and D015 carry: every new model is a release of `protocol` or a plugin, and
  catalogues move fast (467 OpenRouter entries; 9100 commits to models.dev in sixteen months).

### W08 · MEDIUM · The stream has no part boundaries, so completeness is guessed

- **Shape.** The stream format never says that a part has ended; the client decides.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter E.
- **Evidence.** A tool call is complete when its arguments parse as JSON
  (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:984-988`, `:1084-1089`); arguments that do not parse at the end become
  `'{}'` (`:1165-1167`); all reasoning deltas carry `index: 0` (`:776-783`); a signature that
  arrives after text has begun exists only on the finish event (`:822-828`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** Signatures lost in streaming, #423 and #453. A reasoning summary fragmented into one
  entry per delta, which "breaks multi-turn round-tripping when re-submitted" (#519,
  `aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:781-783`). Trailing whitespace re-emitted a finished tool call
  (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:1078-1083`).
- **Does the plan share it?** No, for Anthropic Messages, which opens and closes each block.
  P02-I02 ("built from the deltas alone") and D009 (a complete-or-cut mark) are the cure, and
  replacing cut arguments with `{}` is what D009 forbids. MEDIUM because the shapes are to be
  checked "against … OpenAI chat on paper" (P02-I02), a format in which nothing closes a part.
- **What kuib should do.** In that paper check, write who emits "part closed" when the provider
  does not (the translation, at the next part's first delta or at the last frame), and that a
  tool call whose arguments never closed is marked cut and never repaired. A payload delta must
  address a part by identity, since it can arrive after that part's text has ended.

### W09 · MEDIUM · A capability row is an unverified claim that something else can overwrite

- **Shape.** Facts about how a model behaves at a provider are typed in by hand or copied by a
  script, as booleans and free values, with nothing tying a value to the observation behind it.
- **Subject and axis.** models.dev B, G, H and J; its consumer.
- **Evidence.** Capabilities are booleans (`attachment`, `reasoning`, `tool_call`,
  `structured_output`, `temperature`; `md:packages/core/src/schema.ts:256-271`). Sync scripts
  fill gaps with guesses: `tool_call: existing?.tool_call ?? true`
  (`md:packages/core/src/sync/providers/hyper.ts:201`), "Absent sampling-parameter info, assume
  temperature is tunable" (`md:packages/core/src/sync/providers/chutes.ts:96-97`), an output
  limit that falls back to the context window (`md:packages/core/src/sync/providers/openrouter.ts:253-256`).
  The sync runs hourly and merges itself when churn is small (`md:.github/workflows/sync-models.yml:5`,
  `:151-152`; `md:packages/core/src/sync/auto-merge.ts:4-6`); other changes are reviewed by a
  model told "You cannot fetch citation URLs" (`md:.opencode/agent/pr-reviewer.md:51`). CI
  checks shape, never facts (`md:packages/core/script/validate.ts:16-17`). What the schema
  cannot say is written in comments, which "Sync … drops" except a leading block
  (`md:AGENTS.md:180`). The consumer does not trust the rows: opencode's transform has 78 lines
  testing a model id substring, 144 testing a package name and 5 gating on `release_date`
  (`oc:packages/opencode/src/provider/transform.ts`, for example `:532-541`, `:970`).
  CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** Of 9100 non-merge commits in sixteen months, 1434 have a subject matching fix,
  correct, wrong, incorrect, typo or revert; 304 of those concern context, limits, cost or
  tokens and 422 concern reasoning, tool calls, temperature, attachments or structured output
  (`md` history). Authored values are erased by the sync: "restore MiMo reasoning_options after
  sync wipe" (`md@e0b2ffd`). An empty `reasoning_options` was "hiding /variants in OpenCode"
  (`md@1c1df31`). "OpenAI temperature flags are wrong for several gpt-5 models" (`md@ed3785b`).
  A limit true at one provider was applied to all through the shared base: "scope Kimi K3 1M
  output to Moonshot API" (`md@4594532`). Copilot's Claude limits were reported wrong on
  2026-02-11, one `context` value where the provider states a window and a separate prompt
  limit (#858, still open). A GitHub search of the tracker on 2026-10-08 returned 527 issues
  whose title says wrong, incorrect, outdated or missing.
- **Does the plan share it?** Partly. A full catalogue is a non-goal, and P04-I03 has the right
  rule for one row: "The MiMo row's values come from its captures, not from its documentation."
  Not stated: that every switch on every row is tied to its evidence, or what notices when a
  provider changes; P05-I02's live test checks only that a step closes.
- **What kuib should do.** Have each switch on an offering row cite the fixture that shows it,
  and say for each whether it was measured, taken from documentation or assumed (an enum). Make
  P05-I02 assert each switch of each offering. This is the journal's claim-and-evidence habit
  applied to rows.

### W10 · MEDIUM · The position of an instruction is not part of the format

- **Shape.** A system message is a role in a list, and each provider's table decides what a
  system message that is not first becomes.
- **Subject and axis.** Portkey C.
- **Evidence.** For Google every system message is skipped while building the turns and only the
  first message is considered for `systemInstruction`
  (`gw:src/providers/google/chatComplete.ts:229-236`, `:338-364`). For Anthropic every system
  message is hoisted to the top wherever it sat (`gw:src/providers/anthropic/chatComplete.ts:330-360`).
  One input, two outcomes, neither reported. CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** "the request succeeds and the client cannot tell that the instruction (or the
  assistant's text) was removed" (#1838, open, 2026-10-07).
- **Does the plan share it?** No. D016 makes position part of what is asked, P04-I01 has
  "placing an instruction (system role, user text) with its lifetime", and P01-I02 records the
  case on Claude and its rejection. MEDIUM because the two variants named leave out the third
  thing a provider can force, hoisting, which changes the cached prefix (INFERRED), and because
  nothing yet says a placement other than the one asked is reported.
- **What kuib should do.** Report every placement that differs from where the instruction was
  asked (W02). Add a hoisting variant only when a fixture needs one.

### W11 · MEDIUM · Failures are classified by HTTP status while many arrive inside a 200

- **Shape.** Retry and classification read the status line; the provider's actual error is in a
  body or in a frame of a stream that already answered 200.
- **Subject and axis.** Portkey F and E; OpenRouter F.
- **Evidence.** Portkey decides retry and fallback on the status alone
  (`gw:src/handlers/handlerUtils.ts:676-688`, `gw:src/handlers/retryHandler.ts:103`); a throw in a
  chunk transform is logged and the stream closed cleanly (`gw:src/handlers/streamHandler.ts:376-380`).
  OpenRouter answers 200 once generation starts and sends errors as data events
  (`ordocs:api_reference/errors-and-debugging`); its client must look for "HTTP 200 with error
  payload" (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:268-285`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** A dropped connection gave "a raw `TypeError: terminated` with no HTTP status code, no
  error code, and no response body" and no finish event (#412). Errors reached users as
  "Provider returned error" with the upstream detail buried (#422,
  `aisdk:src/schemas/error-response.ts`, comment on `extractErrorMessage`).
- **Does the plan share it?** Partly. P03-I02 puts classification in transport ("by kind, HTTP
  status, whether a retry can help and after how long") and P04-I02 gives "a provider error
  mid-stream" to the translation. Two places classify, and only the translation can read a
  provider's error body.
- **What kuib should do.** One classification shape in `protocol`, filled by transport for what
  only it sees (connection, status, retry-after) and by the translation for what is in a body
  or a frame. A mid-stream error closes the step as failed with the same classification a
  pre-stream one would get.

### W12 · LOW · A router chooses again, on every request, who serves the model

- **Shape.** The party serving a conversation is decided per request by price, load or failure.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter A and I; Helicone and LiteLLM proxy (secondary).
- **Evidence.** OpenRouter's affinity is a hash of the first system and first non-system
  message, expires after ten minutes, and without `session_id` starts only after a cache hit
  (`ordocs:guides/best-practices/prompt-caching`); the maintainer: "provider sticky-routing
  affinity and cache TTL are intentionally decoupled" (#499). "any error can trigger the use of
  a fallback model" (`ordocs:guides/routing/model-fallbacks`). Helicone fails over on 400 and
  401 as well (`docs.helicone.ai/gateway/provider-routing`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** With a one-hour cache mark, requests flipped provider after gaps over five minutes:
  about $0.005 for a cache hit against $0.04 for the flipped request (#499). Signatures rejected
  after a flip (W01).
- **Does the plan share it?** No. Automatic choice between providers is a non-goal and a session
  holds its provider (D004). A warning for the engine: a change of offering inside a session
  resets the cache and is a boundary for payloads, so it should be an explicit, recorded act.

### W13 · LOW · A mode flag decides whether provider data exists in the output

- **Shape.** One boolean switches the response between "the common format" and "the common
  format plus what the provider really said".
- **Subject and axis.** Portkey B and D.
- **Evidence.** `strictOpenAiCompliance` appears on 203 lines in 38 files; thinking blocks and
  signatures are returned only when it is off (`gw:src/providers/anthropic/chatComplete.ts:597-601`);
  with it off `finish_reason` carries the provider's raw string in the field that otherwise
  holds the normalised one (`gw:src/providers/utils.ts:78`); the gateway defaults to on
  (`gw:src/handlers/services/requestContext.ts:100-107`) while "By default
  strict_open_ai_compliance=false in Portkey Python and Node SDK"
  (`portkey.ai/docs/product/ai-gateway/strict-open-ai-compliance`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** A caller on the default cannot continue a tool round with thinking, since the
  signature was never returned (INFERRED). Gemini and Anthropic thinking came back in different
  shapes (#1112).
- **Does the plan share it?** No: one format, the payload always carried. A warning for any
  later compatibility switch on an offering: it may choose a rendering, never whether data is
  kept.

### W14 · LOW · A provider's tool-call id is the key

- **Shape.** The id a provider gave a tool call identifies the call in the client.
- **Subject and axis.** OpenRouter D; Portkey D; models.dev's consumer.
- **Evidence.** "Some providers return empty, null, or duplicate IDs for parallel tool calls",
  so the client generates its own (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:377-388`, `:954-961`). Portkey invents
  `'portkey-' + crypto.randomUUID()` for Gemini and answers with `'gateway-tool-filler-name'`
  (`gw:src/providers/google/chatComplete.ts:256`, `:660`). opencode rewrites ids before sending
  them to Claude, keyed on `model.api.id.includes("claude")`
  (`oc:packages/opencode/src/provider/transform.ts:224-232`). CONFIRMED.
- **Harm.** "`toolCallId` always starts with 0, causing each tool call across step to 'step' on
  each other", open from 2025-08-15 to 2026-04-13 (#160).
- **Does the plan share it?** No (D013). One consequence to carry into W01: an id issued by one
  offering may not be acceptable to another, so what is sent back on a change of offering is
  rendered from kuib's own identity, the same way every time so the cached prefix holds.

### OpenRouter

**A. Identity.** A model is a free string, `export type OpenRouterChatModelId = string`
(`aisdk:src/types/openrouter-chat-settings.ts:16`). The string carries, at once: the author
and family (`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`); a second, dated identity, since `canonical_slug`
differs from `id` in 315 of 467 entries (`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` is
`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5-20260928`; `orapi:models`); a tier as a suffix, 74 `:batch` and 16
`:free` entries, each with its own limits and price; a routing policy as a suffix (`:nitro`,
`:floor`, `:exacto`), accepted on every id and listed nowhere ("`GET /api/v1/models` … is not
an exhaustive list of every model string a request can use"); a moving alias as a prefix and
suffix, 18 `~author/family-latest` entries; and a second model list in `models`. Suffixes
"can be combined in any order", "the last one in the ID determines the sort"
(`ordocs:guides/routing/model-variants/overview`). Who serves the model is not in the string:
it is chosen per request, or steered by `provider.order`, `only`, `ignore`, with the region
inside the endpoint tag (`google-vertex/us`, `google-vertex/europe`, `azure/us`;
`orapi:models/anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5/endpoints`). CONFIRMED.

**B. Differences.** They live in the closed router; the client sees none of the code. What
leaks into the client: two comparisons on the reasoning `format` and one on a model id
(`aisdk:src/chat/convert-to-openrouter-chat-messages.ts:312-319`, `aisdk:src/provider.ts:351`),
and 45 regression tests named after issues (`aisdk:e2e/issues/`). Capabilities are data per
endpoint (`supported_parameters`, `supports_tool_choice` as four booleans,
`supports_implicit_caching`, `quantization`, `max_completion_tokens`), while the model row
states the best case: `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` lists 20 parameters and 943718 output
tokens, its 29 endpoints support 11 to 20 parameters and 131072 to 943718 output tokens, 8 do
not support `tool_choice: required`, and 1 has implicit caching (`orapi`). CONFIRMED.

**C. What is asked.** One OpenAI-shaped body. Cache intent is Anthropic's vocabulary,
`cache_control: { type: 'ephemeral' }`, in three placements (per block, top level for
"automatic caching", and OpenAI's `prompt_cache_breakpoint`); "TTLs are not translated — a
`cache_control` `ttl` is dropped toward OpenAI" (`ordocs:guides/best-practices/prompt-caching`).
Reasoning intent is one object holding either `effort` or `max_tokens`, converted between the
two by fixed shares of `max_tokens` (high about 80%, medium 50%, low 20%;
`ordocs:guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens`). A change of effort in the middle of a
conversation exists in three wire forms and is rejected on a model that lacks it. Message roles
are `user | assistant | system` (`ordocs:api_reference/overview`); nothing found on what a
system message in the middle becomes for each provider. CONFIRMED.

**D. Opaque provider data.** `reasoning_details`, an array of `reasoning.text` (with
`signature`), `reasoning.summary` and `reasoning.encrypted` (with `data`), each tagged with a
`format` naming the upstream wire family (nine values in `tssdk:src/models/reasoningformat.ts:11-19`,
"anthropic-claude-v1 (default)"). It must be returned unmodified and in sequence. The client
keeps it in three places and reads them in priority order, message, first tool call, first
reasoning part (`aisdk:src/chat/convert-to-openrouter-chat-messages.ts:268-281`). The tag names
a format, not who issued the data: Gemini signatures "are provider-scoped, so they can't be
validated by a different upstream provider", and a continuation routed from Vertex to AI Studio
fails with 400 (#491, maintainer, 2026-06-17). CONFIRMED.

**E. The stream.** OpenAI chat chunks. Parts have no boundaries: a tool call is treated as
complete when its arguments parse as JSON (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:984-988`, `:1084-1089`), and
arguments that do not parse at the end are replaced by `'{}'` (`:1165-1167`). Reasoning deltas
"all carry the same `index` (frequently `index: 0`), so `index` cannot be used to reassemble
the array" (`:776-783`). A signature can arrive after text has started, and is then available
only from the finish event (`:822-828`). The terminal `finish_reason` appears twice, because
OpenRouter "intentionally deviates" from OpenAI's usage chunk (`ordocs:api_reference/streaming`).
A dropped connection used to bypass `transform` and `flush` entirely (#412,
`aisdk:e2e/issues/issue-412-mid-stream-termination.test.ts:5-9`). Unknown reasoning detail types
are turned into `null` and filtered out (`aisdk:src/schemas/reasoning-details.ts:58-65`).
CONFIRMED.

**F. Endings, usage, errors.** Five normalised finish reasons with `native_finish_reason`
beside them (`ordocs:api_reference/overview`); the client maps four and calls the rest `other`
(`aisdk:src/utils/map-finish-reason.ts:14-26`), then rewrites the result from the content: `stop`
with tool calls and encrypted reasoning becomes `tool-calls`, `other` with tool calls becomes
`tool-calls` (`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:455-475`). Usage reports `cached_tokens` and
`cache_write_tokens`; the client computes "no cache" as total minus cache reads
(`aisdk:src/utils/compute-token-usage.ts:27`). Once generation starts the status is 200 and an
error arrives in the body or as a data event; a 500 has its message "replaced with a generic
string" (`ordocs:api_reference/errors-and-debugging`). OpenRouter retries by changing provider or
model; "any error can trigger the use of a fallback model", including context-length and
moderation errors (`ordocs:guides/routing/model-fallbacks`). CONFIRMED.

**G. Unsupported features.** Three answers in one product. Default: "the parameter is ignored"
(`ordocs:api_reference/overview`), since `require_parameters` defaults to `false`. On a
`~latest` alias: the reasoning parameter is remapped to "the nearest supported value". For a
change of effort in the middle of a conversation: "rejected with a 400 instead of being sent
with the update silently dropped" (`ordocs:guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens`). CONFIRMED.
Nothing was found in the pages read that tells the caller, in the response, that a parameter
was ignored or remapped.

**H. Extension.** None for a format or a transform: the router is closed. A third party adds a
key (BYOK) or, on the Enterprise plan, a private endpoint of a model that is already in the
catalogue (`ordocs:guides/routing/private-models`). In the client the smallest unit is a field
spread into the body (`...this.config.extraBody, ...this.settings.extraBody`,
`aisdk:src/chat/index.ts:176-177`), untyped. CONFIRMED.

**I. Control of the bytes.** The caller never builds the upstream request. The only view of it
is `debug.echo_upstream_body`, which "only works with streaming"
(`ordocs:api_reference/errors-and-debugging`); it is how a user found that a Gemini cache mark
was dropped whenever `tools` were present (#508). Re-serialised tool arguments changed key
order and broke the cache (#171, `aisdk:e2e/issues/issue-171-cache-tool-key-ordering.test.ts:5-13`).
CONFIRMED.

**J. Evolution and cost.** Slug features retired in place: `:thinking` and `:online` deprecated,
`:extended` deprecated with "no model currently offers it" and requests that "will fail"
(`ordocs:guides/routing/model-variants/extended`). The client's 3.0.0 is breaking for its host's
reasons, not its own (`ai@^7`, Node 22, ESM only; `aisdk:CHANGELOG.md:13-21`). Response schemas
were closed, broke on new fields, and were opened: "Add `.passthrough()` to all zod object
schemas to prevent validation failures" (`aisdk:CHANGELOG.md:657-659`). The generated SDK makes
every inbound enum open (`tssdk:src/types/enums.ts:11-23`). Tests are hand-written chunk arrays
plus live end-to-end runs; no recorded traffic was found. CONFIRMED.

### Portkey gateway

Source lines were located by a read-only sub-agent and reopened before being cited here.

**A. Identity.** Provider and model are two free strings joined only at request time. The

**B. Differences.** In transform tables per provider plus branches. Counted in non-test source:

CONFIRMED (counts by the sub-agent's greps, the flag count rerun by me).

**C. What is asked.** The OpenAI chat body, widened with optional fields named after one

CONFIRMED.

**D. Opaque provider data.** Kept only when the caller turns compliance off: `...(!strictOpenAiCompliance
&& { content_blocks: response.content.filter((item) => item.type !== 'tool_use') })`
(`gw:src/providers/anthropic/chatComplete.ts:597-601`); in the default mode the thinking blocks
and their signatures are discarded. Three placements for one idea: Anthropic `content_blocks`,
Vertex-Anthropic puts thinking in `content` and filters out `redacted_thinking`
(`gw:src/providers/google-vertex-ai/chatComplete.ts:848-855`), Gemini puts `thought_signature`

(`gw:src/providers/anthropic/chatComplete.ts:143-161`). No code checks which provider produced

`pause_turn`, Google `MALFORMED_FUNCTION_CALL` and `RECITATION`, DeepSeek

**G. Unsupported features.** Dropped without a word. The request is built by walking the
provider's table, not the request (`for (const configParam in providerConfig) { … if
(configParam in params)`, `gw:src/services/transformToProviderRequest.ts:83-92`), so a parameter
with no entry is never visited. The only rejection is an endpoint with no table at all
(`:157-159`). Out-of-range values are clamped silently (`:52-69`). Nothing reports a dropped
parameter to the caller: not found. Open issues: #1579 (cache marks stripped for Claude on

**H. Extension.** One static object literal of about 70 providers
(`gw:src/providers/index.ts:78-153`), typed `[key: string]: any`
(`gw:src/providers/types.ts:149-151`), and a second hand-kept list `VALID_PROVIDERS`
(`gw:src/globals.ts:117-192`) that has drifted from it. No runtime registration: not found. The
smallest unit that can be added is a whole provider, by editing the repository; a model or a

## What they do that this plan lacks

1. **A provider's own stop reason kept beside the normalised one.** OpenRouter returns
   `finish_reason` and `native_finish_reason` on every choice (`ordocs:api_reference/overview`).
   The plan's closed step has one of five endings and no raw reason (W04).
2. **One typed error vocabulary for failures before and during a stream.** OpenRouter's
   `error_type` "describe[s] what went wrong whether the provider error arrives in a
   non-streaming response body or as a mid-stream SSE event"
   (`ordocs:api_reference/errors-and-debugging`). The plan classifies in transport and handles
   mid-stream errors in the translation (W11).
3. **A caller-set strictness for unsupported intents.** `require_parameters`, and a 400 for an
   update a model cannot take (`ordocs:guides/routing/provider-selection`,
   `ordocs:guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens`). D003 has one behaviour, degrade and report
   (W02).
4. **Model facts written once, with a row keeping only what differs.** models.dev's
   `base_model`: "Provider fields win over model metadata during generation"
   (`md:README.md:128`), with a list of paths to drop (`base_model_omit`). The plan has a model
   enum and offering rows, and no record for what is true of a model wherever it is served; with
   two rows that costs nothing, with twenty it is twenty copies.
5. **A retirement date on what is served.** OpenRouter's `expiration_date`, "Deprecation date
   for the model endpoint" (`ordocs:guides/overview/models`). An enum member in `protocol` has no
   lifecycle: nothing says what a stored session does when its model is retired.
